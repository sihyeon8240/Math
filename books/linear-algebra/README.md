# Linear Algebra

More abstract treatment of linear algebra than applied linear algebra (MTH203).
Tools such as matrices, vector spaces and linear transformations, bases
and coordinates, eigenvalues and eigenvectors and their applications.
Characteristic and minimal polynomial. Similarity transformations:
Diagonalization and Jordan forms over arbitrary fields. Schur form and
spectral theorem for normal matrices. Quadratic forms and Hermitian
matrices: variational characterization of the eigenvalues, inertia
theorems. Singular value decomposition, generalized inverse, projections,
and applications. Positive matrices, Perron-Frobenius theorem. Markov
chains and stochastic matrices. M-matrices. Structured matrices
(Toeplitz, Hankel, Hessenberg). Matrices and optimization.
